About Better OS

Better OS is an optional dashboard built into The Wish Ledger. Music files are stored locally in your browser's IndexedDB. Notes and settings stay on this browser. Wikipedia search needs an internet connection. The startup screen is tied to real initialization tasks: fonts, browser database, music library, notes and Wikipedia availability.
